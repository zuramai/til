### What happens when we are sleep deprived?

- Increased risk of cognitive decline and dementia.
- Slower reaction time
- Fatigue
- Trouble thinking and remembering
- Irritability due to degraded emotional regulation
- Increased mental health disorders such as anxiety and depression
- Loss of productivity

Then, what's the amount of sleep needed? The sleep duration is vary depends on the age:

- Newborns (up to 3 months): 14 to 17 hours
- Infants (3 to 12 months): 12 to 16 hours
- Young children (1 to 5 year): 10 to 14 hours
- Children (6 to 12 year): 9 to 12 hours
- Teenager (13 to 18 year): 8 to 10 hours
- Adults (18 year and up): 7 to 9 hours
