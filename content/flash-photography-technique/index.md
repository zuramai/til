To make the background still visible while highlighting the subject while using the flash, lower the shutter speed (like 1/15 or 1/30) and increase the ISO, allowing the lens to catch the ambient light in the background longer. Ambient light is a light that is naturally or artificially exists in the room without adding flash or strobes.
