Write for a specific person. Youn should imagine you're telling someone at a specific knowledge level. It could be yourself three years ago or your friend.

If you're writing about database optimization at advanced level, you don't tell how to do `GROUP BY` cuz it's their everyday meal already.

You have to imagine what kind of reader that would benefits from your writing.
