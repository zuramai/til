Popcorn came from corn kernel, a hard shell type of corn. When heated, the water turns to steam, being pressured until the kernel pops into a fluffy snack.

The corn kernel is from a different type of corn. While sweet corn we usually eat is soft, it's harvested when fully mature and dry. We can't pop a sweet corn because the kernel missing a hard hull and it needs internal moisture that creates the explosion when making pop corn.

## Is popcorn healthy?

Yes. But what makes it unhealthy is the additive such as sweetener and sodium. We can use a minimal amount of seasoning to make it healthier.
