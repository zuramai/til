Metacognition is the process of thinking about owns thinking, the human ability to step back, observe, reflect their own thought process. 

Most people have metacognition ability, though the metacognitive skill vary from person to person. A common metacognition example is when people reflect on their thoughts, gauge their decisions, and notice when they are distacted or unsure.
