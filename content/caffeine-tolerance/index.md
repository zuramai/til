Caffeine consumption causes our brain to release adenosine receptors that prevents you to feel tired. Over the time, the brain releases more receptors as the caffeine consumption increases, which is why the tolerance increases.

The first time you drink coffee, once a day is enough, but after a year, you might feel like you need to consume coffee thrice a day.
