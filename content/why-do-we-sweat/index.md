Sweating is our body response to stabilize body temperature back to 36-37 celcius. It's called thermoregulation, control core body temperature and trigger sweat.

<br />

**Why do we need to sweat after all? Why don't just leave our body "hot"?** 

Because if the core temperature rises to 40C, our body begins to malfunction. 

1. Cellular damage: high temperature break down cells and causing tissue destruction to the whole body.
2. Brain dysfunction: our brain is sensitive to heat. The symptoms progress from confusion, dizziness, lost of consciousness, and coma.
3. Organ dehydration
4. Etc

<br />

And sweating helps to cool our body by **evaporative cooling**, when the sweat evaporates, it absorbs and draw the heat away from our skin, which cool down our blood and bringing our body temperature down.
