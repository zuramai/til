Lately, Ireland has become the [15th coal-free country](https://www.pv-magazine.com/2025/06/20/ireland-coal-free-ends-coal-power-generation-moneypoint/) in the world. In 2024, Ireland generated 37% of its energy from wind, which equals to 11.4 TWh (Terawatt hour).

Here's the complete timeline of the countries in Europe that exits coal: https://beyondfossilfuels.org/europes-coal-exit. 

Europe is aiming for net-zero emissions by 2050. They'll replace it by renewable energy that reduces greenhouse gases and air pollution.
