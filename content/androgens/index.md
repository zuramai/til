Androgens is a group of steroid hormones that maintains male physical health.

<br />

Common types of androgen:

1. Testosterone

   Produced mostly in male testicles, in the ovaries for female, and in the adrenal glands for everyone.
2. Dihydrotestosterone (DHT)

   A stronger androgen converted from testosterone that drives the development of male external genitalia, facial hair growth.

<br />

Androgens also become the main culprit of acne production. These are the steps of how they are linked:

1. Sebum overproduction. Androgens (specifically testosterone) are connected to sebocytes (the main oil-producing cells) and stimulates the sebaceous glands to produce more excess sebum.
2. Follicular hyperkeratinization. Androgens alter the shedding skin inside the follicle wall. Keratinocytes (primary cell type of epidermis) are stick together instead of sloughing off. This creates microcomedone.
3. Bacterial proliferation. Excess sebum trapped behind the plug and creates a rich nutrient environment where C. acnes multiply rapidly.
4. Inflammation. In reaction to the bacteria overgrowth in the cells, your immune system send white blood cells to the infected area and triggers inflammatory pustules, papules, nodules, and cysts.
