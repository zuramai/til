When your stomach is empty, especially after you wake up in the morning, don't eat carbs first. Eat protein or fiber. Food order is important. A study found that if you fill your stomach with carbs before protein and vegetables when it's empty, 73% more sugar was released into the blood compared to the reverse order.

And another fact that previous studies have shown that premeal ingestion of whey protein reduces postmeal glucose level.
