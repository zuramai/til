This article is dedicated for the idea of the yearly Devover Meetup event, which is a night hangout in an Airbnb while having a deep talks to get to know each other better.

Because we're all tech guys who prefer activities that is not _exaggerated_ or _alay_, we cut off activities such as karaoke or something _bertele-tele_ or smth like that.

So the activities will be:

1. Dinner
2. Ice breaking, throwing up random funny question to anyone, making sure everyone knows each other
3. Sharing session, deep talks, giving each other perspectives on anything
4. Barbercues and chit chats
5. Gaming, PS5/Nintendo Switch
6. Coffee counter (we don't drink cocktail here)
7. Door Prize mystery
8. Confession jar
9. Es buah bar
