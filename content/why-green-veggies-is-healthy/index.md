Chrolophyll is a natural green pigment that naturally founds in plants, that absorbs sunlight into energy. The darker the green color, the higher the chlorophyll content.

Chlorophyll is also good for humans, it contains antioxidants, which prevents damage from free radicals. A study suggests that eating green vegetables daily can lower the risk of dementia, and slow the decline of memory and cognitive function.

Chlorophyll is fat-soluble, so adding fat in the meal is good for you.
