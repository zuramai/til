Corticosteroid is a synthetic steroid medication that can help reduce inflammation in your body. We can use corticosteroid to treat a lot of health problems such as Lupus, Asthma, Eczema. We can also treat an injury like using steroid.

Some of the most common types:

1. Prednisone
2. Cortisone
3. Hydrocortisone
4. Methylprednisone

It's the fastest way to reduce inflammation in your body. It can also be the shape of injections like cortisone shots, which targeting the inflammation.

The side effects goes up the longer you take them. Increased appetite, muscle weakness, skin changes, weight gain, are among the side effects of corticosteroids.
