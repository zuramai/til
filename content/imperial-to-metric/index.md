US is still using imperial measurement system while the rest of the world is mostly using the metric system. Not exactly an imperial tho, they're using U.S. Customary System, a derived system from the older British Imperial System.

## Imperial (US) vs Metric conversion

**Weight:**

- 1 ounce (oz) = 28,3495g
- 1 pound (lb) = 16oz = 454g
- 1 teaspoon (tsp) = 4,92892ml
- 1 ton = 2000 lbs

**Volume:**

- 1 fl oz = 29,5735ml
- 1 cup = 8 fl oz = 236,588 ml
- 1 pint = 2 cup = 1/8 gallon = 473,175 ml
- 1 quart = 2 pint = 946,353 ml
- 1 gallon = 4 quarts = 3,78 litre

**Length:**

- 1 inch = 2,54 cm
- 1 feet = 12 inch = 30,48 cm
- 1 yard = 3 ft = 0,9144 meter
- 1 mile = 5280 ft = 1,60934 km

**Temperature:**

- Celcius = (Fahrenheit − 32) × 5/9
