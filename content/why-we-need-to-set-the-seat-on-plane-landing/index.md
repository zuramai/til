It might sounds trivial but actually important reasons why we need to have the seat upright during takeoff or landing.

1. Locked stability, because reclined seats can swing or snap under heavy crash forces. Prevent injuries.
2. Knee protection for the person behind
3. Helps passenger to lean forward safely into the correct brace if hard landing happens.
4. Less head movement
5. Allow fast emergency exits
