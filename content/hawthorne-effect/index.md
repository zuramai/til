Hawthrone effect is when our behavior changes because we're aware of being watched or being observed (rather than due to the environment), trying to be better or improving performance.

For example, among medical staff, if they're not being watched, they aren't washing their hands. But when there's a study of hand-washing there, the compliance of hand-washing is 55% more than usual.
