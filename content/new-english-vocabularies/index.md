I wrote few english vocabularies that I learned today, either new vocabularies or new usage of the vocabularies.

1. Vomit (v)

- Vomiting all information ()
- Vomiting toxic waste into water

2. Unwitting (adj)

- Unwittingly signed on contract
- Unwitting mistakes

3. Stalemate

- Kebuntuan
- When a competition is standstill because neither side can make a move to win

4. Dragged on

- Explains the process of **lengthy** processes (The meeting dragged on for two hours)
- Explains physical effort (I dragged on my suitcase)

5. Jumpy

- Anxious/uneasy (The voice is a little bit jumpy)

6. Inquisitive

- Inclined to ask questions; curious (The child is very inquisitive; in an inquisitive voice)

7. Insinuation

- A subtle, indirect malicious hint or suggestion (*I've done nothing to deserve your vicious insinuation*)

8. Rapport

- A close and harmonious connection or relationship with people with mutual understanding and trust. (*The teacher developed a strong rapport with her students*)
